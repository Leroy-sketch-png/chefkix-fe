# Q118 Immediate Content Availability Plan

## Goal

Make already-loaded Taste Profile insight and Recipe Detail content available
immediately, especially the primary cooking command, without flattening motion
that communicates loading, progress, rank, or completion.

## Order

1. Map every delayed start in both routes and their route-owned content children.
2. Remove delayed starts from ordinary loaded facts, insight, proof, and commands.
3. Preserve transition duration, hover feedback, loading skeleton sequencing,
   leaderboard spectacle, celebrations, and global reduced-motion ownership.
4. Lock those boundaries with a focused source contract.
5. Run focused, application, production-build, source-sweep, and runtime gates.

## Risks

- A broad animation cleanup could erase meaningful loading or celebration timing.
- Simultaneous entry could reveal overlap or horizontal overflow at runtime.
- A route-owned child could retain hidden latency after the page owner is fixed.
- Authenticated Taste Profile evidence may remain unavailable without a valid
  current browser session; that limitation must remain explicit.
